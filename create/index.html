I was tasked with designing the user experience of receiving lab results in a virtual healthcare context. I was able to work through discovery, ideation, feedback, refining, hi-fi build, and user feedback to arrive at a solution that matched our use case and exceeded user expectations.

As a prescription device with a physical world component, the Teal Wand presents a bundle of interesting constraints and challenges. I built a digital experience that guides our users to the end of their screening journey, receiving results and necessary follow-up care. Along the way they have a video visit with a provider, receive their kit in the mail and mail their sample to the lab, and in some cases require an in-person follow-up with a local provider to investigate an abnormal result.
